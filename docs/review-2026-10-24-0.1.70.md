## Yellow Argon correction

The official [violation guide](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/troubleshooting/#keyword-stuffing) identifies Yellow Argon as keyword stuffing. Its [spam FAQ](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/spam-faq/#keyword-spam) addresses lists of supported brands. The previous provider enumeration has been replaced with a description of local servers and compatible hosted services in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Galician and Catalan. Provider compatibility details remain in technical documentation and actual settings.

`scripts/validate-store-materials.mjs` checks all eight public listing fields and current submission versions. With `--artifacts`, it compares listing bytes across sources, kit ZIP and extracted files, compares the embedded copy-page JSON, verifies submitted documents and the extension ZIP, and checks extracted-file SHA-256 sums. Packaging calls source validation automatically. These checks guard this project's recorded regression; they do not certify store compliance or acceptance.

The copy-page generator now refreshes unchanged generated files from a newer kit. Before writing, it checks conflicting existing files against their recorded hashes and stops if publisher edits are present. It preserves private reviewer edits rather than silently overwriting them or serving stale listing text.
