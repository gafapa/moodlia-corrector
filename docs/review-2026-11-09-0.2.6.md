# Extension review: 0.2.6

Date: 9 November 2026. 0.2.6 fixes **Correct when moving to the next student**;
the panel, extraction and privacy behaviour are unchanged. Counts and the
archive checksum are in [`review-2026-11-09-0.2.6.json`](review-2026-11-09-0.2.6.json).

## What changed

| Area | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting the next correction | Moodle's grader loads the next student in steps (the user, then the submission and files). Each step reset the panel, and only the first reset saw a new student, so the later resets dropped the pending correction and nothing started. | The pending start is kept across resets for the same student and runs once the student's data is read. |
| Students skipped | Only students corrected earlier on the page. | Also students who already have a grade in Moodle's form: a direct grade, a scale value, a selected rubric level or a marking guide score. The status line says why. |

The browser check for this option now loads the next student in steps, as
Moodle does, and visits a student who already has a grade. Against the 0.2.5
code the check stops at the automatic correction; with 0.2.6 it passes.
